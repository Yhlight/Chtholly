# Code of Conduct

Chtholly contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful, focused, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain tradeoffs and evidence.
- Help newcomers find the right document, test, or issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.

Maintainers may moderate issues, pull requests, and discussions to keep the project healthy.
